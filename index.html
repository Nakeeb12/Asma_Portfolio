About

Dependable by habit, curious by training.

I studied psychology at Middlesex College and then Felician University, which taught me to listen carefully, notice patterns and treat people with patience. I've put that to work in two very different settings: behind the front desk of a counseling practice and at the register of a busy warehouse store.

At Clear Conscience Counseling I handled the administrative side of care: keeping records in order, matching young clients with clinicians, and maintaining files in New Jersey's CYBER system. Before that, two and a half years at Sam's Club taught me to stay friendly and accurate under pressure, and to train the people working beside me.

I get things done on time, whether I'm working alone or with a team, and the people I work with often point to how well I juggle several tasks at once. I'm looking for a role where I can keep learning and help an organization reach its goals.
